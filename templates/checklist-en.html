Wedding Checklist

For {{bride}} & {{groom}}

12 Months Before

  • Set overall budget
  • Create guest list (rough draft)
  • Choose wedding date
  • Book venue (ceremony + reception)
  • Hire wedding planner (if using one)
  • Start researching vendors

9-10 Months Before

  • Book photographer/videographer
  • Book caterer
  • Choose wedding party
  • Start shopping for wedding dress
  • Book officiant

6-8 Months Before

  • Order wedding dress
  • Book florist
  • Book DJ/band/music
  • Book transportation
  • Send save-the-dates
  • Book hotel blocks for guests
  • Plan honeymoon

4-5 Months Before

  • Order invitations
  • Choose groomsmen attire
  • Order wedding cake
  • Book hair/makeup trial
  • Register for gifts
  • Buy wedding rings

2-3 Months Before

  • Send invitations
  • Finalize menu with caterer
  • Get marriage license info
  • Write vows
  • Final dress fitting
  • Confirm vendor details

1 Month Before

  • Get marriage license
  • Confirm final guest count
  • Create seating chart
  • Finalize timeline with vendors
  • Pick up wedding attire

1-2 Weeks Before

  • Confirm final headcount
  • Pack for honeymoon
  • Final payments to vendors
  • Delegate day-of tasks
  • Rehearsal & rehearsal dinner

Wedding Day

  • Eat breakfast
  • Give rings to best man/maid of honor
  • Enjoy your day!
